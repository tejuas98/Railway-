Proposed Solution: GATI-SETU (गति-सेतु)

Dynamic Real-Time Expected Time of Arrival (ETA) Engine for Coaching Trains across Indian Railways
Ministry of Railways
Problem ID: 26028
Smart India Hackathon 2026
1. Live RTIS Satellite GPS Telemetry Fusion
Ingests ISRO NavIC/GAGAN 30s locomotive pings via Kafka and applies an Extended Kalman Filter to snap coordinates to 1D track chainage, eliminating drift and phantom jumps.
2. Dynamic e-Caution & TSR Speed Parser
Parses civil engineering T/409 electronic caution orders in real-time, calculating exact kinematic deceleration, 20-30 km/h crawl, and rake-length acceleration recovery penalties.
3. Level Crossing Gate & Interlocking Tracker
Monitors station S&T relay data loggers for road traffic gate-closure delays, proactively injecting signal halt penalties before loco pilots encounter red aspects.
4. Physics-Informed Kinematic Running Engine
Replaces static timetables with continuous Newton-Davis physics equations factoring in locomotive horsepower (WAP-7 vs WAG-9), coach trailing tonnage, and track gradients.
5. Spatio-Temporal Graph Headway (ST-GAT)
Simulates 4-aspect signal progression and models preceding freight headway lag using Spatio-Temporal Graph Attention Networks to dynamically propagate upstream delays.
6. Multi-Day Journey Cascading Predictor
Uses an autoregressive path-loss model that anticipates downstream cross-zone loop-line overtakes once a long-distance multi-day train loses its scheduled timetable slot.
7. Spatial & Temporal Variability ML Engine
Deploys online LightGBM regressors trained on 1.5M+ runs to capture peak suburban rush, weekly freight surges, and Ghat incline crawls with daily automated retraining.
8. Adverse Weather & Visibility (FSD) Adapter
Ingests live IMD Doppler radar feeds, automatically enforcing the statutory General Rule 3.61 Fog Safe 60 km/h speed ceiling during dense winter fog conditions.
9. Terminal Platform Queuing & Outer Hold Detector
Applies Markovian queueing to track platform clearance, alerting passengers and dispatchers when trains are stabled at outer home signals awaiting platform evacuation.
10. Station Turnaround, Cleaning & Pit-Line Sync
Broadcasts ±2 min high-precision countdowns 45 minutes ahead, pre-staging OBHS cleaning squads, water replenishment, and pit-line maintenance slots for on-time return rakes.
11. Loco Crew 10-Hour Duty Watchdog (HOER)
Tracks pilot running hours against the statutory 10-hour HOER limit via CMS, alerting controllers 90 minutes early to arrange relief crews and avoid mid-line stalls.
12. Downstream Feeder Transport & Logistics Bridge
Exposes sub-25ms REST & WebSocket APIs with 90% confidence bounds [P10, P90] to synchronize city cabs (Ola/Uber), state buses, metros, and parcel cargo logistics.
