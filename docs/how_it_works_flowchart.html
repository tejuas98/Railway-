How it Works
1. Live Train & Signal Tracking
Pulls 30s ISRO NavIC satellite GPS, track signals & freight feeds
2. Pinpoint Track Alignment
Snaps GPS to exact 1D railway track chainage to stop jumping & drift
3. Track Repair & Slowdown Zones
Reads civil caution orders (T/409 TSR) & calculates exact deceleration delay
4. Fog & Weather Safety Limits
Enforces mandatory 60 km/h fog speed limit (GR 3.61) & wet rail grip
5. Preceding Trains & Engine Power
Accounts for slower freight trains ahead, engine horsepower & coach weight
6. Station Platform Availability
Checks if platform is free to calculate exact outer signal hold countdown
7. Honest Arrival Time & Delay Reason
Delivers calibrated arrival window (P10-P90) & plain-English delay cause
