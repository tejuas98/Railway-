How It Works

Passenger enters train number (e.g. 12301) or selects upcoming journey
↓
2. Live Track & Signal Scan
System checks live satellite GPS, preceding freight trains, and track signals
↓
3. Ground Realities Check

↓
4. Dynamic ETA Calculation
Calculates true realistic arrival time and exact outer signal hold countdown
↓
5. Transparent Output on App & Display
Passenger sees honest arrival time, countdown to platform entry, and plain reason for any delay
